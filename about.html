About Geumdo High School

OUR STORY

Fifty years of the golden path

Founded in 1974, Geumdo High School ("금도" — golden path) began as a single building with three classrooms. Today we are a full campus serving over 1,200 students across three grade levels, known across Seoul for our academic results and our strong extracurricular tradition.

We believe a school's job is not only to prepare students for university entrance exams, but to help them become thoughtful, capable people — which is why every student here belongs to at least one club, sport, or ensemble alongside their academic track.

MISSION & VALUES

What guides us every day

Honor

We hold ourselves to a shared code of integrity — in the classroom, on the field, and in how we treat one another.

Wisdom

We chase understanding, not just marks — encouraging curiosity, discussion, and independent thought in every subject.

Unity

Homeroom families, club teams, and grade cohorts are built to last well beyond graduation day.

A message from the Principal
