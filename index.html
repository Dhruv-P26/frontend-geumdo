WHY GEUMDO

An education built on three pillars

Our name, "Geumdo" — meaning golden path — reflects our promise: every student leaves with a clear direction and the character to walk it.

Academic Rigor

A national-curriculum-aligned program with STEM, humanities, and language tracks, taught by subject specialists in small, focused classes.

Arts & Athletics

From the choir and taekwondo team to the robotics club, students build discipline and creativity well beyond the classroom.

Community & Care

Homeroom mentors, a full counseling office, and an active parents' association keep every student supported and seen.

CAMPUS LIFE

A campus built for learning and belonging

Our courtyard, library, gymnasium, and science wing sit around the central quad — the same quad where morning assembly and the autumn festival have taken place for five decades.
